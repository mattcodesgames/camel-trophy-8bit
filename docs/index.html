ZX SPECTRUM 48KCamel Trophy Game

Aquarius Edizioni, Italy, 1985 · Simone Majocchi, Giorgio Arcangeli, Eugenio Ciceri, Bruno Molteni · now in English
▶ Play

How it plays

Italy's first big commercial Spectrum game arrives on one tape in three parts, and each part loads the next when you need it:

  1. Qualifying quiz: 32 questions on off-road mechanics, survival, navigation and first aid. Your score earns you a crew code.
  2. Route selection: Zaire, the Amazon, Borneo, or plan your own ten days in D.I.Y. mode.
  3. Driving game: rebuild your Land Rover each morning, then tackle fords, humps, bridge building and jungle forks.

The original is in Italian. This edition translates every screen into English, including captions and maps drawn into the pictures, by patching the original tape. The Italian tape is kept untouched in the repository.

COMMODORE 64Camel Trophy Video Game

Tequila, 1985 · joystick, one player
▶ Play

How it plays

A run of short arcade challenges set on the expedition:

  1. Supply drop: release the crate from the plane and steer its parachute onto the target.
  2. Jungle drive: keep the Land Rover clear of the bushes.
  3. Refuelling: waggle the joystick to fill the tank before time runs out.
  4. Bridge crossing and a rough-track run to the finish.

The disk image is a cracked release by CFB; its cracker intro is replaced by a new title and credits screen written in 6502 assembly. The game itself is unchanged.

A bit of history

The Camel Trophy was an annual off-road expedition, held from 1980 to 2000 and famous for its sand-yellow Land Rovers.

1984

Simone Majocchi sketches a Camel Trophy computer game in Milan, working with the team around the Italian tape magazine Run.

1985

The Spectrum master tape goes for duplication on 16 September 1985. A national radio campaign starts on 25 September, and the game sells for Lit. 12,000.

1985–86

Tequila releases the Commodore 64 Camel Trophy Video Game, a set of short joystick challenges. It's catalogued on Lemon64 and CSDb.

Under the bonnet

Both emulators are small, dependency-free C programs compiled to WebAssembly with Zig.

ZX Spectrum 48K
  • Z80 core with all documented and undocumented opcodes and flags
  • Edge-accurate TZX playback, so the original turbo loader runs unmodified
  • Translation tool rebuilds the BASIC, patches machine-code text and re-encodes the compressed route maps
Commodore 64 (PAL)
  • 6510, VIC-II with sprites and raster IRQs, two CIAs, and a 3-voice SID with filter
  • No Commodore ROMs: a small replacement kernel with trapped calls, plus an original font
  • New title screen with a sprite-multiplexed badge, raster bars and a SID tune
